HOW TO READ THIS
C / U / R / E / L / M = Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic.
Enemy "body damage" is the contact hit: 0.25 × enemy damage, once every 0.7s. Total body health is the sum of every part at that tier. Core health is the central part only. Turn speed is radians per second; lower is slower. Speeds are world units per second.
Player weapon damage is shown at 100% craft efficiency and a base cannon damage of 10. Multiply by efficiency and by st.dmg for your actual build. Every crafted weapon has a 2.5× critical hit and a 2.2× Overdrive multiplier.

CORE FORMULAS
• Base cannon damage: 10 × 1.2^Cannon Damage level.
• Base cannon fire rate: 4 + 0.7 × Fire Rate level shots/s.
• Base cannon shots per trigger: 1 + Extra Barrels level.
• Base cannon range: 760u; projectile speed 900u/s.
• Crafted weapon cooldown: base cooldown ÷ (0.7 + 0.3 × efficiency). At 30% efficiency the cooldown is 1.27× longer.
• Player hull: 100 × 1.22^Hull level.
• Enemy damage: 16 × tier multiplier. Common 16 · Uncommon 48 · Rare 144 · Epic 432 · Legendary 1,296 · Mythic 3,888.
• Enemy health: part health × tier multiplier. Common ×1 · Uncommon ×4 · Rare ×18 · Epic ×80 · Legendary ×360 · Mythic ×1,600.

DAMAGE TYPES
• Kinetic — base cannon, scatter driver, heavy machine gun, rail sniper, swarm drones. Enemy energy panels stop it dead where they are; the panel takes no damage, so aim at the gaps. Hull shells still stop the rail.
• Energy — pulse turret, arc lance, shield-bypass laser. Passes straight through enemy energy-shield panels; the bypass laser also ignores the panel entirely.
• Explosive — seeker pod, homing missile launcher, flak cannon, mine layer, cluster rockets. Stopped dead by enemy energy-shield panels.
• Shock — EMP cannon, chain lightning, gravity bomb, sonic disruptor. Passes through panels; direct area effects skip the panel check anyway. EMP, chain and sonic skip hull shells.
• Corrosive — acid sprayer, venom injector. Stopped dead by enemy energy-shield panels.
• Thermal — plasma cutter. Passes straight through enemy energy-shield panels.
• Physical — contact, knockback and direct impulses. Collisions are not projectiles, so the panels never check them; physical rounds are stopped.

ENEMY DEFENCE RULES
Energy shield: each Shield Generator projects one wall just outside the torso armour plates — a curved arc for flyers and serpents, a straight bar for walkers, some species sweeping them at 0.4-0.7 rad/s. A panel covers only a slice of the ring (2 at epic, 3 at legendary, 4 at mythical), so the gaps between panels are open weak points. Energy, shock and thermal damage passes through. Every other projectile or beam is stopped at the panel and does nothing to it: it has no charge to strip, so it never drops or re-arms and only disappears when the generator part is destroyed.
Hull shells (armour plating): tier 2 and above carry solid layers holding 60% of the enemy hull each, drawn as steel slabs over the body. A hit is swallowed by the outer layer and only part of it is lost: bullets 30%, missiles and flak 40%, beams 80% (energy is only resisted 20%), area blasts 40%, shock 15%; Legendary legs carry 1 plating layer of their own, mythical legs 2. Breaking a layer releases a damaging shockwave. A mythical tier-5 layer is 60% of its hull, e.g. 943,872 HP on a 1,573,120 hull (four layers).

ENEMIES — 32 SPECIES

Overflying a body: if the ship gets inside an enemy (half its radius, clamped to 45–260u) the body swats it away instantly — 2.2× its contact damage plus a 1,500 + 90×tier u/s shove — with a 1.4s cooldown per enemy. Stunned bodies cannot swat.
Turn speed: common through epic share the species rate; legendary ×0.9, mythical ×0.8. Turning and walking scale with the share of healthy limbs, so broken or crippled legs slow both, and a body with no working legs cannot turn at all.
Legs cripple before they snap: a limb that falls below 35% health stops carrying weight or steering and stops swinging, even though it is still attached. At 50% it still works.

Any species that does not carry a gun or shield generator by design still rolls for one from epic up: gun chance 12% + 6%/tier, shield chance 10% + 5%/tier, so 30%/25% at epic and 42%/35% at mythical. Rolled guns pick rotary, scatter, laser or heavy gear instead of the plain turret, weighted towards the bigger weapons at high tiers.
